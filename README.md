# Plantolex 🌱

**The Agricultural Knowledge Encyclopedia**

Plantolex is a planned bilingual Arabic–English agricultural knowledge platform designed to connect scientific research, reliable references, and practical agricultural knowledge in one accessible place.

It aims to help farmers, agricultural engineers, students, researchers, educators, and agriculture enthusiasts find, understand, and apply agricultural information through an organized encyclopedia, research resources, learning materials, a bilingual glossary, and a community for sharing questions and field experiences.

## 🌍 Vision

To build a trusted, accessible agricultural knowledge hub that bridges the gap between scientific research and real-world agricultural practice across languages, experience levels, and agricultural contexts.

## 🔎 The Problem

- Agricultural knowledge is scattered across websites, research papers, institutional publications, and social media.
- Online recommendations may lack reliable sources or overlook important differences in crops, growing conditions, and local regulations.
- Finding and understanding relevant scientific research can be challenging, especially for beginners.
- Language barriers can limit access to valuable agricultural knowledge.
- Users need clearer ways to distinguish documented information, community experiences, and advice reviewed by qualified specialists.

## 📚 Core Sections

1. **Agricultural Encyclopedia:** Structured articles covering crops, plant health, pests and diseases, integrated pest management (IPM), biological control, soil, plant nutrition, irrigation, protected agriculture, hydroponics, seeds, genetics, and relevant food-safety topics.
2. **Ask the Agri Community:** A planned question-and-answer space where users can describe agricultural problems, share relevant photos and field observations, and learn from community members and qualified specialists.
3. **Research & References:** An organized directory of scientific papers, university resources, extension guides, official publications, and legally accessible books, with links to original sources whenever available.
4. **Learning Hub:** Beginner-friendly explanations, educational resources, and structured learning paths for different agricultural subjects and experience levels.
5. **Agricultural Glossary:** An Arabic–English glossary of agricultural and scientific terminology, including clear definitions, scientific names where relevant, and links to related topics.
6. **Source-Grounded Knowledge:** A planned system for finding relevant information in trusted references and presenting answers with traceable sources, appropriate context, and clear statements of uncertainty.

## ✨ What Makes Plantolex Different?

Plantolex aims to bring structured agricultural knowledge, scientific references, and community experience together while keeping their roles distinct.

- Reference-based information is linked to its supporting sources.
- Community contributions are identified as user-shared experiences or answers.
- Specialist-reviewed content is labeled as reviewed only after an actual review.
- Automated responses are treated as informational assistance, not confirmed diagnoses or substitutes for qualified professional judgment.

## 🛡️ Guiding Principles

- **Evidence before certainty:** Claims should be supported by appropriate sources whenever possible.
- **Transparency:** Sources, publication details, update dates, and review status should be shown where available.
- **No guessing:** When essential information is missing, the platform should communicate uncertainty or request clarification.
- **Responsible recommendations:** Plant-protection advice should consider the crop, pest or disease, local registration, label instructions, and applicable regulations.
- **Clear review status:** Content must never be presented as specialist-approved unless the review has actually taken place.
- **Continuous improvement:** Users should have a way to report errors and suggest corrections.
- **Respect for intellectual property:** Research materials should be linked, cited, or shared only in ways permitted by their licenses and applicable law.

## 🌐 Languages & Accessibility

Plantolex is planned as an Arabic–English platform from the beginning, with:

- Right-to-left (RTL) Arabic and left-to-right (LTR) English layouts.
- A clear language switch and language-appropriate navigation.
- Search designed to support Arabic and English terms.
- Scientific names and standard terminology where they improve accuracy.
- Responsive layouts for mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

## 🧭 Development Roadmap

Development will proceed in stages:

1. **Foundation:** Establish the project structure and build a responsive static website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
2. **Core Content:** Publish a carefully selected collection of agricultural articles and properly documented references.
3. **Discovery Tools:** Develop article pages, category navigation, search, and the agricultural glossary.
4. **Bilingual Experience:** Implement and test Arabic–English navigation, RTL/LTR layouts, and language-aware content.
5. **Community Features:** Evaluate suitable backend services for user accounts, questions, answers, and image uploads.
6. **Source-Grounded Assistance:** Explore ways to retrieve relevant information from trusted references and present citations and uncertainty clearly.
7. **Testing & Public Beta:** Test usability, accessibility, content quality, and performance, then improve the platform using feedback.

The roadmap may change as technical requirements, available resources, and user needs become clearer.

## 💚 Cost & Sustainability Strategy

Plantolex will prioritize free and open-source tools, independent backups, and suitable free hosting during early development. External services will be selected only after reviewing their limitations, terms, privacy implications, and potential costs.

Paid services will be considered only when a demonstrated need justifies the expense. The project will avoid unnecessary dependencies on a single development tool or service.
